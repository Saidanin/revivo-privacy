Privacy Policy — Revivo

Revivo: Colorize Old Photos · Last updated: 30 October 2026
The short version

Your photos never leave your phone. Revivo colorizes photos directly on your device. We do not upload, collect, or store your photos, and we do not require an account.

1. Who we are

Revivo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile application that adds color to black & white and old photos. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

2. Your photos

All photo colorizing happens on your device. When you choose a photo, it is processed locally by the app and the colorized result is saved only where you choose to save it (your device gallery). Your photos are never sent to us or to any server, and we never see, collect, or store them.

3. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name or email. The app itself does not gather your photos or contacts.

Some information is collected automatically by third-party services we use, as described below.

4. Advertising

Revivo shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect certain information such as your device’s advertising ID, general device information, and app-activity related to ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policies. You can learn more and manage your choices here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data (AdMob)

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Purchases

Revivo offers an optional paid upgrade (Revivo Pro). Payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card or payment details — Google handles all payment information.

6. Children

Revivo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ads network, please contact us.

7. Data security & retention

Because colorizing runs on your device and we do not collect your photos, there is no photo data for us to store or secure on a server. Any ad-related data is handled by Google as described above.

8. Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID, opt out of personalized ads in your device settings, and delete the app at any time to stop all data collection by the app and its ad provider.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above when we do.
